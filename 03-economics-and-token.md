## Protocol Economics

LazaiTrader operates on a revolutionary **pure profit-sharing revenue model** that perfectly aligns platform success with user profitability. Unlike traditional platforms that charge upfront fees or subscriptions, LazaiTrader is completely free to use and generates revenue exclusively through **15% of user profits** upon withdrawal.

**Proven Performance Foundation:**
- **Live Trading Results:** 82.15% ROI over 95 days (documented and verifiable)
- **Total Trades:** 126 real-world executions with net positive outcomes
- **Backtesting:** 5 years of validated performance on ETH-USDC pairs
- **Operating Costs:** Ultra-low at just $30/month, enabling sustainable operations

**Revenue Model Advantages:**
- **Zero Barrier Entry:** Users pay $0 to start trading
- **Perfect Incentive Alignment:** Platform only profits when users profit
- **Ultra-Low Risk:** Break-even achieved with just 15 profitable users
- **High Trust Factor:** Complete transparency builds user confidence

## Token Role

LazaiTrader intentionally does not utilize a native token, focusing on sustainable business fundamentals rather than tokenomics complexity. This approach provides:

- **Immediate Accessibility:** No token purchase requirements or liquidity concerns
- **Regulatory Simplicity:** Operates as a pure software service without token-related complications
- **User Focus:** All development resources directed toward trading performance rather than token mechanisms
- **Sustainable Growth:** Revenue directly tied to user success rather than speculative token value
